## 2. Strict Non-Refundable Policy on Paid Plans

Due to the digital and instant nature of Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) delivery—including instant tenant subdomain provisioning, automated database partitioning, cloud infrastructure allocation, and immediate license key activation:

### 2.1 No Refunds Upon Activation
Once a payment is successfully processed and the corresponding subscription (Monthly Starter, Bi-Annual Pro, Annual Enterprise, or Lifetime Unlimited) is activated, **all fees paid are 100% final and strictly non-refundable**.

### 2.2 No Prorated or Partial Period Refunds
We do not issue partial refunds, prorated credits, or fee adjustments for:
- Early cancellation before the billing cycle completes.
- Mid-period downgrades between tiers.
- Inactivity, non-usage, or abandonment of your workspace during an active subscription period.

### 2.3 Lifetime Unlimited Deal Terms
The **Lifetime Unlimited Plan** provides permanent, non-recurring software usage rights. Because the full commercial value and perpetual software license are delivered immediately upon account provisioning, Lifetime Deal purchases are **strictly and irrevocably non-refundable**.

## 3. Subscription Cancellation Terms

You have the complete freedom to cancel your subscription renewal at any time.

### 3.1 Cancellation Process
You may cancel your subscription renewal by:
1. Submitting a cancellation request to our billing support at [info@prohitcoretech.com](mailto:info@prohitcoretech.com) with your tenant subdomain and registered email.
2. Managing your subscription settings inside your workspace administration dashboard prior to the subsequent renewal date.

### 3.2 Access After Cancellation
When you cancel an active subscription:
- **No Further Charges:** Your payment method will not be billed for subsequent billing cycles.
- **Active Until Cycle Expiry:** Your workspace remains completely functional with full access to all features until the final date of your current pre-paid period.
- **Non-Refundable Notice:** Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle; no refund is provided for the remaining days of the active period.

## 4. Technical Payment Failures & Duplicate Deductions

The only recognized exception to our non-refundable policy is a verified **Technical Duplicate Payment**:

### 4.1 What Constitutes a Duplicate Payment
If, due to a network timeout, browser refresh, or payment gateway anomaly, your bank account or card is debited more than once for the exact same order and subscription transaction.

### 4.2 Resolution Process
1. Contact billing support within **48 hours** of the transaction at [info@prohitcoretech.com](mailto:info@prohitcoretech.com).
2. Provide your registered business email, tenant subdomain, transaction date, and the Unique Transaction Reference (**UTR**) or payment gateway reference ID for both deductions.
3. Upon technical verification that a duplicate charge occurred, the redundant payment will be refunded to the original payment source within **5 to 7 business days** (subject to your bank's processing cycles).
